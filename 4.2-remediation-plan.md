These come from reading the sources and data through 6 Sep, not from anything the data proves. Each one needs checking as the project runs.

1. **No owner since 12 Aug.** Ping tickets started release day, Wen Li was away 14–24 Aug, Priya left 21 Aug, and 83 tickets are open. The data stops at 6 Sep, so today's picture is unknown. *Verify:* fresh extract from Ravi; ticket status from Nadia.

3. **Rank penalty may be self-reinforcing.** If missed pings lower rank, a responder who falls behind can't recover, and only Wen Li knows how ranking works. *Verify:* Wen Li on penalty size and duration; document ranking.

7. **Revenue and retention risk.** Billing is per active responder and responders are independent. Starved responders may leave. Nobody has linked tickets to billing. *Verify:* ask Finance about churn and the duplicate seat in ticket 3075.
8. **Confidentiality risk from per-responder analysis.** Slicing by responder and pairing with handler names and transcripts needs care; Policy 4.1 is contractual. *Verify:* get the Policy 4.1 owner's sign-off before building new per-responder reports.

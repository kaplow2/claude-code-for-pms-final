# 02 · Super Hearing — prompts

**Context:** You joined Rook two weeks ago as PM on Dispatch.
Release 4.2 shipped on 12 August, just before you arrived, and
landed badly. Last session you built a context file and met the
company.

You still do not know what actually went wrong.

At the end of the session, type wrap up, and Claude Code saves the
prompts you wrote yourself below — not the starter prompt — updates
CLAUDE.md, and saves your work to GitHub. By Module 6 this file is a
prompt library built from your own questions.

---

### 1.

Now that we've identified themes, what are the unique items? things mentioned by only one person in the interviews?

### 2.

what data sources do we have or need to substantiate these themes in the interviews?

### 3.

Based on the interviews, what is the #1 issue with 4.2? Have we seen this issue before?

### 4.

Clarify what is meant by keyword. Is this a specific field on the tickets? and if so, does that change the theme counts?

### 5.

### 6.

What are the key observations in ticket activity pre- and post- August 12? What are the unexpected spikes post Aug. 12? What are new issues vs. what is in line with historical data? What should I be paying attention to / concerned about?

### 7.

Let's also look at who filed tickets? I'm looking for differences in personnel behavior that we should consider in evaluating ticket activity

### 8.

do we have data on close rates? or other support ticket metrics?

### 9.

You've now read both folders (interviews and tickets). Where do they disagree? What's loud in the interviews but rare in the tickets, and what's all over the tickets that nobody brought up in the interviews?

### 10.

With the qualitative and quantitative issue reports (interviews & tickets), we have vastly different themes/activity. How should we trust and/or reconcile this information?

### 11.

What additional data sources do we have or need that could add validity to these data points?

### 12.
